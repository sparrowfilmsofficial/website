## Core Philosophy — Why Sparrow Films Is Different
**We always look for emotion.** Across all of human history, people have always connected through emotion — not just information. This is the single biggest thing that sets Sparrow Films apart from a typical content or production agency.

- We don't just make videos that "look good" — every project starts by finding the emotional core of the story first, before any camera is picked up.
- This is why our tagline is **"Crafted for Emotion"** — it's not a slogan, it's the actual production philosophy behind every reel, ad, and brand film we make.
- This emotional-first approach is also *why* our content performs well: audiences stop scrolling and stay engaged when something makes them *feel* something, not just see something. This directly ties into our 64% average 3-second hook retention rate.
- Whether it's a 30-second reel or a 10-minute brand documentary, the question we always start with is: **"What emotion should the viewer feel by the end of this?"**

## Policies

### Payment Terms
- **50% advance before the shoot, 50% at the time of delivery.**

### Turnaround Time
- Standard delivery: **within 7 business days** (7–10 days depending on complexity).
- Complex projects may take longer.
- **Urgent/rush delivery**: available at an added cost — customer should **contact the sales department** for urgent-turnaround pricing.

### Revisions & Feedback Policy
- **3 rounds of revisions included** per reel/deliverable.
- **72-hour feedback window**: client must give feedback within 72 hours of receiving a draft, or it is automatically considered approved and final.
- The standard 7–10 day delivery window depends on the client giving feedback on time; Sparrow Films is not responsible for delays caused by late client feedback.
- **Extra revisions** (beyond 3 rounds, or after the 72-hour window closes): billed at **₹499 per revision** (₹999 if the project is complex).

### Travel & Outstation Shoots
- **No travel fee** for shoots within **Delhi and Noida.**
- For shoots **outside Delhi/Noida**, the client covers all travel, fuel, and toll costs once the shoot team reaches the decided location, until the shoot is completed.

### Logo Placement & Watermarking
- A **Sparrow Films watermark/preview mark** is placed on draft videos during the review process.
- The watermark is **removed only after final payment is received**, and the client gets the clean, final-quality file.
- This is separate from adding the *client's own brand logo* into a video — that can be arranged as part of the project if requested; confirm with the sales team.

### Services We Do Not Currently Offer
- **Graphic Design**: standalone graphic/poster/print design outside of video content is not offered. Poster/thumbnail design is only available as an add-on (₹1,499 per poster/thumbnail) tied to a video we've shot for the client — not as a standalone graphic design service.
